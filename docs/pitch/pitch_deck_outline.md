# Pitch Deck Outline

> Two decks: the **Round-1 idea submission deck** (common in Indian hackathons for shortlisting) and the **final pitch deck**. Keep slides visual, with ≤15 words of body text per slide. Owner: B.

---

## A. Round-1 idea submission deck (if there's a shortlisting round)

Usually 6–10 slides, judged without us in the room, so it must stand on its own.

| # | Slide | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title | Project name, tagline, team name, members, PS ID/track |
| 2 | Problem | Who suffers, how badly (**one cited stat**), why existing solutions fail |
| 3 | Proposed solution | One-liner + 3 key features, with a simple visual or mock screen |
| 4 | How it works | Flow/architecture diagram: user → app → AI/data → output |
| 5 | Uniqueness / innovation | What's novel vs. 2–3 named alternatives (comparison table) |
| 6 | Tech stack | Logos grouped by frontend / backend / AI / data / deploy |
| 7 | Feasibility & risks | Why it's buildable in the time; data sources; key risks + mitigations |
| 8 | Impact & benefits | Users reached, time/₹ saved, social/environmental impact; scalability path |
| 9 | Mockups / prototype | Wireframes or early screens (Figma is fine) |
| 10 | References | Data sources, research papers, APIs |

---

## B. Final pitch deck (3–5 min live + demo)

| # | Slide | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook** | 0:20 | A stat, a story, or a question about a real person's painful moment. No "Hi, we are team X" opener. |
| 2 | **Problem** | 0:25 | Who, how often, what it costs. One number, big font. |
| 3 | **Solution one-liner** | 0:15 | "We help ___ do ___ by ___." Product name + logo. |
| 4 | **LIVE DEMO** | 1:30–2:00 | Switch to the app. Follow [demo_script.md](demo_script.md). Hit the wow moment. |
| 5 | **How it works** | 0:30 | Architecture diagram; point to the 1–2 technically hard parts. |
| 6 | **Why it's different** | 0:20 | vs. alternatives; our unfair advantage / novel angle. |
| 7 | **Impact** | 0:20 | Quantified; who benefits; scalability / business model (one line). |
| 8 | **What's next** | 0:10 | Roadmap: 2–3 bullets. |
| 9 | **Team + ask/close** | 0:10 | Names + roles, repo/demo QR code, memorable closing line that echoes the hook. |

**Rules of thumb**
- Demo > slides. If time is cut, cut slides 6–8, never the demo.
- Every claim gets a number or a source.
- One idea per slide, big fonts (≥24pt), high contrast, consistent colors with the app.
- Put a QR code to the live app/repo on the last slide.
- Backup: the demo video is embedded or linked on slide 4, ready to click.
